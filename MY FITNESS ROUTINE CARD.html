My Fitness Routine Cards

This is a simple fitness routine card layout that you can use to plan your daily workouts. Each card represents a different activity in your fitness routine, along with the recommended time for each activity.

Morning Stretch

Start your day with a gentle stretch to wake up your muscles and improve flexibility.

Time: 45 minutes

Physical Workout

Engage in a physical workout session that includes cardio exercises like jumping jacks, running in place, or cycling.

Time: 1 hour

Muscle Build-up

Practise squats, push-ups, and planks to build body strength.

Time: 30 minutes

Yoga Session

Try simple yoga poses to improve balance, flexibility, and focus.

Time: 15 minutes

Evening Walk

Go for a relaxing walk to stay active and refresh your mind.

Time: 25 minutes

Rest and Hydrate

Drink enough water and take proper rest after completing your workout.

Time: All day

Note: Remember to consult with a fitness professional before starting any new workout routine, especially if you have any pre-existing health conditions.
